\# Design Notes - Cycloidal Gearbox 1:10



\## Key dimensions for SolidWorks



\### Housing

\- Outer diameter : 60 mm

\- Height : 35 mm

\- Wall thickness : 3 mm

\- Ring pin circle radius (R) : 25 mm

\- Ring pin diameter : 4 mm

\- Number of ring pins : 11



\### Cycloidal disc

\- Number of lobes : 10

\- Eccentricity (e) : 2 mm

\- Thickness : 6 mm

\- Output pin holes : 10 × ⌀6 mm on ⌀30 mm circle



\### Output flange

\- Diameter : 50 mm

\- Thickness : 8 mm

\- Output pins : 10 × ⌀4 mm



\### Input shaft (servo MG996R)

\- Spline : 25T (standard MG996R output)

\- Eccentric offset : 2 mm



\## Modelling order in SolidWorks



1\. Housing with ring pin bores

2\. Cycloidal disc profile (equation-driven curve)

3\. Eccentric input shaft

4\. Output flange + pins

5\. Assembly
